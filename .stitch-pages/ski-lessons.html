Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from first-time desert skiers.

How long are the lessons?

expand_more

Our standard introductory lessons run for 2 hours. We find this is the perfect amount of time for beginners to learn fundamental skills before fatigue sets in.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

expand_more

No, you do not need to own equipment. While lesson prices do not include gear rental, we partner with local Phoenix and Tucson shops to secure rental packages for our students.

Where do we meet for the lesson?

expand_more

We utilize designated learning areas. Specific meeting instructions will be provided upon booking, ensuring a stress-free arrival process away from crowded main resort areas.
