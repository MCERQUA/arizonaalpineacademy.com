# Book a Lesson

**H1:** Book a Lesson
**Subhead:** Tell us your experience level and which resort you're headed to, and we'll set you up with the right lesson — private or group, adult or kids, whatever fits your trip.

## What Happens After You Book

We'll reach out — usually within one business day — to confirm your experience level, your preferred resort, and timing that works for your trip. From there, we'll recommend private or group instruction based on what you're looking for, walk you through what a lesson actually covers, and make sure you know what to expect before you show up. There's no obligation at the request stage — this is the start of a conversation about what you actually need, not a locked-in commitment.

## Who This Is For

Whether you've never touched a pair of skis or a snowboard, you tried once years ago and want a real refresher, or you're booking for your kids and want to be there to help, tell us where you're starting from. There's no experience level too basic — "I've genuinely never done this" is the most common starting point we hear, and it's exactly who this academy is built for.

## What to Have Ready

Knowing your experience level, which resort you're planning to visit (or whether you want our recommendation), and roughly when your trip is happening helps us give you the right lesson format faster. If you're booking for kids, their age helps us match the right teaching approach. You don't need every detail nailed down to request a lesson — even a rough idea of your plans is enough to get started, and we'll fill in the rest together.

## Private or Group — We'll Help You Decide

If you're not sure whether a private or group lesson is the better fit, that's a normal question and part of what we'll help you sort out. Private lessons move at your exact pace and work well for nervous beginners, tight timelines, or small groups who want full attention. Group lessons put you with other beginners at a similar stage and tend to cost less per person — a good option if you're comfortable learning alongside others. Tell us your preference, or tell us you're not sure, and we'll recommend what fits.

## Timing Matters More Than You Might Think

Lessons book up faster than people expect, especially around weekends, holidays, and any stretch right after a fresh snowfall — the same stretches when the resorts themselves are busiest. If you already have a trip date in mind, requesting a lesson as early as you can gives you the most flexibility on timing and format. If you don't have a firm date yet, that's fine too — we can get the fundamentals in place ahead of time so you're ready whenever your trip actually happens.
