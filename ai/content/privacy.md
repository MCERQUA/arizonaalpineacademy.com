# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 20, 2026

Arizona Alpine Academy ("we," "us") operates arizonaalpineacademy.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site and how we use it.

## Information We Collect

**Information you provide directly.** When you submit a form on this Site — including the Book a Lesson request form or the general contact form — we collect the information you enter, which may include your name, phone number, email address, preferred resort, experience level, and any message or details you provide.

**Automatically collected information.** Like most websites, this Site may collect basic technical information automatically, such as your browser type, general location (derived from IP address), pages visited, and referring website — used to understand how the Site is used and to identify how visitors found us.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to respond to your lesson request or inquiry, confirm scheduling and experience-level details, and provide the requested instruction service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Form Submissions and Third-Party Services

Forms on this Site are processed through our website hosting provider's form-handling service. Submitted information is transmitted securely and used solely for the purposes described above.

## Data Retention

We retain information submitted through this Site for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, schedule a lesson, and maintain appropriate business records.

## Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request corrections, or to request deletion, subject to any legal or legitimate business recordkeeping requirements.

## Children's Privacy

This Site itself is intended for use by parents and adults booking lessons, including lessons for children, and is not directed to children under 13 for independent use. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children through this Site; information about a child provided in connection with a booking is provided by the submitting adult.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
