Have a question about lessons, which resort fits your first trip, or what to expect before you go? Reach out directly, or use the form below and we'll get back to you promptly.

## Before You Reach Out

If your question is about lift tickets, on-mountain rentals, or resort-specific conditions and hours, those questions are best directed to the resort itself — we handle instruction and trip prep, not resort operations. But if you're not sure whether your question is ours to answer or the resort's, just ask — we're happy to point you in the right direction either way.

## What We Can Help With

Most contact-form questions we get fall into a few categories: whether a specific child's age is ready for their first lesson, whether Arizona Snowbowl, Sunrise Park Resort, or Mount Lemmon Ski Valley fits a particular trip better, what to expect if you've never worn ski boots or strapped into a snowboard before, and general questions about how private versus group lessons work. If your question fits somewhere in that list, or somewhere close to it, send it over — there's a good chance we've answered a version of it before and can give you a straight, specific answer rather than a generic one.

## Response Time

We aim to respond to contact form messages within one business day. If your question is time-sensitive — an upcoming weekend trip, for example — calling directly is the fastest way to get an answer before you need it.
