# Topical Map — arizonaalpineacademy.com ("Arizona Alpine Academy")

## Site classification
Trade-service / local-service lead-gen site (NOT insurance). Business: ski & snowboard
instruction and trip-prep for Phoenix-Valley and Tucson residents heading to Arizona's own ski
resorts (Arizona Snowbowl near Flagstaff, Sunrise Park Resort near Greer, Mount Lemmon Ski
Valley near Tucson). Branded as its own local business — "Arizona Alpine Academy" — NOT
Contractors Choice Agency; CCA's canonical phone/email/address are reused only as the operating
contact channel, no CCA/insurance branding anywhere on the site.

## Critical naming note
"Alpine Academy" as a search phrase is a homograph collision with unrelated schools nationwide
(a Utah teen residential-treatment facility, assorted unrelated charter schools/preschools — see
keywords.json critical_finding). The brand name uses "Alpine Academy" (matches the domain) but
NO SEO copy anywhere targets that phrase; all SEO targeting uses the real, verified Arizona
ski/snowboard demand instead (Arizona Snowbowl, Sunrise Park Resort, Mount Lemmon Ski Valley,
"alpine skiing lessons," first-timer/beginner PAA questions).

## Pillar
**arizonaalpineacademy.com/** — homepage. "Learn to Ski or Snowboard in Arizona" — the desert-
to-slopes value prop: most Phoenix/Tucson residents have never skied; this academy teaches them,
preps them for a day trip, and points them to the right AZ resort for their first time.

## Cluster pages (5)
1. **/ski-lessons/** — Private & group ski lessons. Targets "ski lessons flagstaff," "arizona
   snowbowl lessons," "alpine skiing lessons."
2. **/snowboard-lessons/** — Snowboard-specific instruction, kept as its own page since it's a
   genuinely distinct discipline/audience from skiing. Targets "snowboard lessons flagstaff."
3. **/kids-family-ski-programs/** — Kids & family programs. Targets "kids ski lessons arizona,"
   "family ski trip arizona," "junior ski team" (framed as beginner/youth programs, not
   competitive racing — that's explicitly flagstaffskiclub.org's niche, not ours).
4. **/first-time-at-arizona-snowbowl/** — First-timer's guide / day-trip prep for Arizona
   Snowbowl specifically. This is the highest-leverage informational page given "arizona
   snowbowl" carries 40,500/mo — not chasing the navigational term itself, but owning the
   adjacent "is it good for beginners / what do I need to know" layer PAA confirms real demand
   for. Targets "arizona snowbowl," "arizona snowbowl rentals," beginner PAA questions.
5. **/sunrise-mount-lemmon-trip-prep/** — Trip prep for the other two AZ resorts (Sunrise Park
   Resort, Mount Lemmon Ski Valley), combined into one page since each individually carries
   meaningful but secondary volume (18,100 and 8,100/mo respectively) relative to Snowbowl.
   Targets "sunrise park resort," "mount lemmon ski valley," "arizona ski resorts."
